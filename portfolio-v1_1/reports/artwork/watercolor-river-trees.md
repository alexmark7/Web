# Individual forest-matched riverside trees

Updated on 2026-11-12 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The existing autumn forest was the style reference. The replacement sheet contains exactly three individual trees: one green broadleaf, one evergreen conifer, and one copper/ochre broadleaf. Each sprite has one trunk and one tree, with transparent space around it.

## Active artwork and display exports

- [Tree master](../../assets/scenery/watercolor-river-trees.webp): 768 × 256 transparent lossless WebP, three equal square cells. The generated 2170 × 725 PNG was normalized to the master size and shifted up 11px so the visible root tips meet the renderer's 82% trunk-foot anchor.
- [Display atlas](../../images/optimized/watercolor-river-details.webp): 204 × 34 transparent lossless WebP. Three 34 × 34 house cells are followed by three 34 × 34 individual-tree cells.
- [High-density atlas](../../images/optimized/watercolor-river-details@2x.webp): 408 × 68, with the same layout at twice the resolution.

The tree replacement initially preserved the existing house artwork, sizes, locations, and opacity. A subsequent [house architecture cleanup](watercolor-river.md#house-architecture-cleanup) refined the house cells while preserving these individual-tree pixels and their alpha values. Tree display size follows `clamp(16, mountainWidth * 0.022, 27)`, with small variations from 0.82 to 1. Individual crowns remain smaller than the foreground forest for distance.

Twenty-one irregularly spaced trees follow sampled dry ground around the winding far bank and central island. Their stable coordinates vary in both horizontal position and bank depth, and each anchors its visible trunk foot to the land. The previous row at 73–77% river height was removed because it overlapped the dense foreground forest. There are no grouped tree sprites or wide floating ground platforms.

The existing river/forest canvas still combines all scenery into one static painting. No extra image requests, DOM elements, live filters, animation loops, or redraws during scrolling are introduced. Atlas loading and before/after scenery were checked at 1920 × 1080, 1366 × 768, and 390 × 844 at 2× density; decoded house pixels were also compared directly with the previous exports.
