- [distant-birds.svg](distant-birds.svg): seven small hand-drawn M-shaped wing strokes, created directly as SVG for crisp rendering at a distance.

The generated PNGs were encoded as WebP at quality 0.9, preserving transparency. Clouds were resized to 1280 pixels wide for the website. CSS controls placement, opacity and scrolling; the paint texture itself is static. Seven paintings form eight clouds across the viewport and drift at independent speeds, with motion disabled when reduced motion is requested.

The CSS stacking order is faint paper ink, left-hand birds, mountains and the horizontal river with riverside trees, middle cloud (05), sun, then the other seven clouds and cherry tree. All artwork remains behind the page content. The sun sits in the upper-right sky at 96% opacity, with a pale painted mist veil in front. Sun, mist, mountains and river share a gentle 4vh vertical scroll movement. An independent time-based animation sways the sun by 1.5–4px in either direction, taking 10 seconds each way, including while idle. Reduced motion disables the sway and scroll drift. Native scroll-driven CSS and the JavaScript fallback use the same scenery movement. The mountain painting is composited three times at full opacity to build denser pigment while preserving its transparent, feathered edges. Cloud 08 adds a smaller, mirrored version of cloud 04 at the middle right. The light-mode header is transparent at the top; a uniform frosted background and thin divider appear as scrolling starts. The cherry tree is inset from the right edge by 40% of its own width, anchored to the document, and enters view naturally near the footer, without a reveal animation. Ink positions use a repeatable random seed, and the ink layer stays fixed to the viewport while content scrolls. Clouds have measured clearance below the navbar and are placed in the clear sky above or beside the mountains. The ink and tree were also generated with the built-in image tool and encoded as transparent WebP at quality 0.9, with the dimensions listed above.
